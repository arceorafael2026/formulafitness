How to read this report

  • First attribution: the original source recorded by GHL. This report does not substitute latest attribution or the separate Contact Source field.
  • Direct: GHL recorded Direct traffic. This does not establish that the person had no earlier marketing exposure.
  • Paid: 42 Paid Search + 18 Paid Social = 60 records based on GHL classifications. No additional paid contacts are inferred from UTMs.
  • Referral and Social media: retained as GHL labels; Referral is not assumed to mean a member referral, and Social media is kept separate from Paid Social.
  • CRM UI, Other and unknown: retained without reassignment. Unknown / blank means no first-source value in the exported row; none were blank in this export.
  • Contact records: these are CRM records, including any imported, manually created, test, spam or existing-member records present. They are not independently verified qualified enquiries, bookings or sales. Creation month may reflect import timing.

Accuracy checks and remaining reconciliation

  • 429 CSV rows = 429 dashboard records = sum of all monthly and channel totals.
  • All 429 creation dates parsed; all 429 first-source values populated.
  • No identical duplicate rows, repeated nonblank email addresses, or repeated nonblank phone numbers found. Two records have neither email nor phone; unique people cannot be guaranteed without Contact IDs.
  • The earlier standard Contacts CSV has 435 records and timestamped creation dates with UTC offsets. Dashboard dates differ for some records, including across month boundaries. The files were not combined or used interchangeably.
  • Before treating this as the complete CRM acquisition history, reconcile a fresh standard Contacts export by Contact ID against attribution coverage and confirm the account reporting timezone.
