ACTIVE AGING QUESTIONS

Can I start as a beginner?

Yes. Your coach starts with your goals, experience and current ability, and explains each exercise. Tell us about your background when you book.

Can I return to exercise after a break?

Yes. Share your history and what you would like to get back to. Your coach will discuss a suitable way to restart, adjusting as your ability changes.

What training format is available?

Formula Fitness offers one-on-one Personal Training and Semi-Private Training. Your coach will explain which formats are available for Active Aging and which may suit you.

How is scheduling arranged?

Session times are arranged with the studio after your conversation. Choose a consultation time in the calendar; current availability is discussed on the call.

How do I find out about pricing?

Pricing depends on the training option that suits you. Book a consultation call and the studio will discuss suitable options and pricing with you.

Where is the studio?

3971 Ball Rd, Los Alamitos, CA 90720.

What happens after I book?

You choose a time in the calendar, enter your details and receive a booking confirmation. On the call, the studio will talk through your goals and starting point, suitable training options, scheduling and pricing, and explain the next steps.
