PRICING AND FIRST-VISIT QUESTIONS

What training options are available?

Formula Fitness offers one-on-one Personal Training and Semi-Private Training, where you follow your own plan with coach guidance in a small-group environment. Use the cards above to explore each option.

How do I find out about pricing?

Pricing depends on the training option that suits you. Book a free 15-minute consultation call and we will discuss suitable options and pricing with you.

Can I start as a beginner or return after a break?

Yes. Your coach starts with your goals, experience and current ability, then adjusts exercises as your strength and confidence grow. Tell us about your background on the call.

What happens during the consultation call?

On your free 15-minute call we will talk through your goals, your experience, the training options available, current availability and pricing, and what the next steps look like.

Where is the studio?

3971 Ball Rd, Los Alamitos, CA 90720.

What are the current opening hours?

The studio is open Monday to Friday, 5 AM to 10 PM, and Saturday and Sunday, 6 AM to 6 PM. Hours can change, so check the Google listing or ask on your consultation call.
