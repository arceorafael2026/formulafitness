What the InBody 580 Measures

A complete picture of your body composition, so your program starts from real data.

Body fat and fat-free mass

Your exact starting point, so you know what needs to change.

Visceral fat area

The fat around your internal organs.

Segmental lean and fat analysis

See where muscle and fat sit on your right arm, left leg and trunk. If there is an imbalance, your program is built around it.

Phase angle and ECW ratio

Phase angle is an indicator of cellular health. The ECW to total body water ratio can point to swelling or fluid imbalance.

Basal metabolic rate

How many calories your body burns at rest: the starting point for any real nutrition conversation.

InBody score

A single number that sums up your scan, so you have one simple figure to track over time.

INBODY QUESTIONS

What is the InBody 580?

A body composition scanner. It gives a detailed picture of your body fat, fat-free mass, where muscle and fat sit on your body, and more, in about 30 seconds.

How is it different from a DEXA scan?

A DEXA uses radiation. The InBody 580 uses bioelectrical impedance and is non-invasive.

Can I see my results afterwards?

Yes. Every scan syncs to the InBody app on your phone, so you can see your results, track your history and watch your progress over time.
