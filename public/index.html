FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is semi-private training the same as a fitness class?

Semi-private training combines individual programming with coaching in a small group. You follow a plan shaped around your goals rather than everyone completing the same workout.

Can I enquire if I’m a beginner or returning after a break?

Yes. Tell us about your experience and current routine so we can discuss whether semi-private training is a suitable option.

How many people train together, and when are sessions?

Contact us for current group capacity, session times and availability. We’ll help you explore options that fit your routine.

What happens after I submit the form?

Your form sends a consultation enquiry. The Formula Fitness team will contact you to arrange the next step and discuss training options and pricing. Submitting does not confirm an appointment.
